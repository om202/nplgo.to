## Your Neighbor's Shop Has a QR Code. Here's Why Yours Should Too.

Walk through any bazaar in Kathmandu, Pokhara, or Biratnagar and you will notice it: the laminated QR code sticker next to the cash register. The pharmacy has one. The momo shop has one. The kirana pasal on the corner has one.

This is not a trend. It is how Nepal does business now. Over 2.3 million merchant points accept QR payments, and monthly QR transactions exceed NPR 96 billion. Customers expect to scan and pay. When your shop does not have a QR code, you are not just missing a convenience. You are losing sales to the shop next door that does.

The good news: setting up QR payments is free, takes less than a day, and requires nothing more than a smartphone and your existing bank account.

This guide walks you through the exact steps.

## Step 1: Choose Your QR Payment Provider

Nepal has three main paths to accepting QR payments. You do not need to pick just one, but start with the option that fits your situation best.

### Option A: Fonepay (Through Your Bank)

**Best for:** Shops that want to accept payments from the widest range of customers.

Fonepay is not a wallet. It is a network. It connects 180+ banks, finance companies, and digital wallets into a single QR system. When you get a Fonepay merchant QR, customers can pay you from virtually any banking app, eSewa, Khalti, or any Fonepay-connected wallet.

**How to register:**

1. Visit your bank branch (the bank where you have your business or personal account)
2. Ask for Fonepay merchant QR registration
3. Provide your citizenship certificate, PAN/VAT certificate (if registered), and a passport-size photo
4. Your bank processes the application in 2-5 business days
5. You receive your Fonepay merchant QR sticker and merchant ID

**Key advantage:** One QR code accepts payments from almost every digital payment app in Nepal. Indian tourists can also pay via UPI.

### Option B: eSewa Merchant

**Best for:** Shops in areas where eSewa is the dominant wallet.

eSewa is Nepal's largest digital wallet with the biggest user base. An eSewa merchant account lets customers pay you directly from their eSewa balance.

**How to register:**

1. Download the eSewa app or visit the eSewa merchant portal
2. Apply for a merchant account with your business details
3. Submit your citizenship, PAN certificate, and business registration (if applicable)
4. eSewa verifies your application in 1-3 business days
5. You receive your eSewa merchant QR code

**Key advantage:** Largest user base. Strong brand recognition. Many customers specifically ask "eSewa chha?"

### Option C: Khalti Merchant

**Best for:** Shops that want fast onboarding and a tech-forward platform.

Khalti (now merged with IME Pay as IME Khalti) offers merchant onboarding with a streamlined digital process.

**How to register:**

1. Visit the Khalti merchant registration page or contact their business team
2. Fill in your business details and upload documents
3. Submit citizenship and PAN certificate
4. Approval typically takes 1-2 business days
5. Download and print your Khalti merchant QR

**Key advantage:** Fast digital onboarding. Growing user base after the IME Pay merger.

## Step 2: The Single QR vs. Multiple QR Decision

This is the most practical decision you will make, and most guides skip it entirely.

### Multiple QR Codes (Common but Messy)

Many shops end up with 3-4 QR stickers at the counter: one for Fonepay, one for eSewa, one for Khalti, maybe one for their bank. The customer has to ask "kun QR scan garnu?" and you have to point to the right one. It works, but it is slow and confusing.

### Single Fonepay QR (Recommended)

If you register for Fonepay through your bank, one QR code handles payments from all Fonepay-connected apps, which covers the vast majority of digital payment users in Nepal. This is the cleanest setup for a small shop.

### The Practical Hybrid

The setup most small shops end up with, and the one that works best, is:

- **1 Fonepay QR** (covers bank apps + cross-wallet payments)
- **1 eSewa QR** (for customers who specifically want to pay from eSewa balance)

Two QR codes, clearly labeled, covers nearly every customer scenario.

## Step 3: Set Up Your Payment Notification System

Accepting QR payments without a notification system is like having a cash register you cannot open. You need to know instantly when money arrives.

### On Your Phone

- **Enable push notifications** for your bank app, eSewa, and Khalti
- **Turn off Do Not Disturb** during business hours
- **Keep your phone charged** because a dead phone means you cannot confirm payments

### The Confirmation Sound

Most payment apps play a sound when you receive money. This is your new cash register "ding." Make sure it is turned on and loud enough to hear over shop noise. Some merchants connect a small Bluetooth speaker to make the notification audible.

### What to Do at the Counter

1. Customer scans your QR code
2. Customer enters the amount and confirms payment
3. **Wait for your notification** before handing over goods
4. If the notification does not arrive within 30 seconds, ask the customer to check their transaction status

**Never accept a screenshot as proof of payment.** Fraudsters use fake screenshots or old transaction screenshots. Always verify on your own device.

## Step 4: Print and Place Your QR Code

Your QR code's physical setup directly affects how many customers use it.

### Printing Specs

| Specification | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Size | 8-10 cm (for counter placement) |
| Material | Laminated card or vinyl sticker |
| Resolution | 300 DPI minimum |
| Format | PNG from your provider, or SVG for custom prints |
| Color | Black QR on white background (highest scan reliability) |

**Cost:** NPR 20-50 at any local print shop for a laminated QR card. Vinyl stickers are NPR 50-100.

### Where to Place It

- **Next to the cash register**, facing the customer at eye level or slightly below
- **Well-lit area** because QR codes in shadow or under dim lights fail to scan
- **Flat surface** because curved or wrinkled QR codes cause scanning problems
- **Away from other QR codes** so keep at least 10 cm between multiple QR stickers and customers do not scan the wrong one

### What to Print Alongside

Your QR sticker should include:

- The provider logo (Fonepay, eSewa, Khalti) so customers know what to scan with
- Your shop name or merchant name
- A simple instruction: "Scan to Pay" or "QR बाट भुक्तानी गर्नुहोस्"

Most providers give you a ready-made sticker with all of this. If you are printing your own, keep it clean and simple.

## Common Mistakes That Cost You Sales

### 1. No Payment Confirmation System

Relying on the customer to show you their screen is the number one mistake. Set up notifications on your phone and always verify payments on your own device.

### 2. Faded or Damaged QR Sticker

Sunlight, grease, and wear destroy QR codes. Laminate your sticker. Replace it every 3-4 months or whenever you notice scanning issues. A QR code that takes multiple attempts to scan frustrates customers.

### 3. Wrong QR in the Wrong Spot

If you have multiple QR codes, label them clearly. Customers scanning the wrong code, failing, and giving up is a lost sale and a bad experience.

### 4. Phone on Silent or Dead

If you cannot hear or see the payment notification, you will hold up the line asking customers to wait while you check. Keep your phone charged, notifications on, and volume up during business hours.

### 5. Not Training Your Staff

If you have employees at the counter, make sure every one of them knows: which QR is which, how to verify a payment, and what to do if a payment does not come through. A confused staff member is worse than no QR code at all.

### 6. Ignoring the Receipt

For transactions above NPR 1,000, customers may ask for a receipt or reference number. Know how to pull up transaction history in your merchant dashboard. This also helps you reconcile your books at the end of the day.

## How to Handle Refunds and Disputes

QR payments are not like cash. You cannot just hand money back from the register. Each platform has its own refund process:

- **Fonepay:** Refunds are processed through your bank. Contact your bank's merchant support with the transaction ID.
- **eSewa:** Use the eSewa merchant dashboard to initiate a refund, or contact eSewa merchant support.
- **Khalti:** Refunds can be initiated through the Khalti merchant portal or by contacting their support team.

**Tip:** Keep a simple notebook or spreadsheet logging daily QR transactions. When a dispute arises, you can quickly find the transaction ID, amount, and timestamp. This saves hours of back-and-forth with support.

## What QR Payments Mean for Your Books

Every QR transaction creates a digital record. This is a significant upgrade over cash for your bookkeeping:

- **Daily reconciliation:** Check your merchant dashboard at closing time. Match the total with your sales register.
- **Tax compliance:** Digital records make PAN/VAT filing straightforward. Your accountant will thank you.
- **Cash flow visibility:** You can see exactly how much came in through QR payments versus cash, helping you understand customer payment preferences.

Most merchant dashboards let you export transaction history as a CSV or PDF for your records.
